Sound familiar?

These are not accounting problems. They are information problems: the data is already there, but nobody turns it into something you can decide on.

01

Margins are a mystery

You know revenue by customer, but not what you actually keep by product, job or location. Pricing decisions are made on gut feeling.

02

No budget, or one nobody uses

If there is a budget, it is a file made in January that nobody compares with actuals any more. Variances surface at year end.

03

Inventory is a black box

High book value, unknown turnover, slow-moving stock never measured. And every stock count brings surprises.

04

Reports built by hand every month

Someone in accounting spends three days pasting cells. Every month. With all the risk of error that comes with it.

05

The new ERP doesn't give you the numbers

You changed ERP and reporting got worse, not better. The data is in there, but it doesn't come out in the shape you need.

06

The bank or a partner wants data

You need reliable management figures for a credit line, an investor or a due diligence, and nobody in the company produces them.

Frequently asked questions

We're small. Does this make sense for us?

Below one million in revenue, much less than 30 hours a month is often enough: you can start with a smaller scope and a proportionate commitment. The right question is not how much you sell, but whether you know today where you are making money.

Do you replace our accountant?

No, and that is not the goal. Statutory accounts, accounting standards, compliance and tax stay with your adviser: they are not our field and we do not handle them. We work on the management side, on what you need to make decisions. The two sides talk to each other, and working with your accountant is welcome.

Do you use artificial intelligence?

Yes, mainly Claude AI, where it saves time without giving up control: analysing very large accounting extracts, writing queries and automations, finding anomalies and reconciliation gaps. Every number we deliver is still checked and reconciled by hand against your sources. AI speeds up the work; it does not sign off the conclusions.

Can you help us use Claude AI in-house?

Yes. Together we identify the repetitive tasks where AI saves the most time, prepare tailored prompts and templates, and coach your people on real work until they are self-sufficient. We also set simple rules on which data to share, so you can use AI without putting confidentiality at risk.

Do you work on site or remotely?

Mostly remotely, which is what keeps costs low. On-site meetings are concentrated at the start and at key milestones. For companies in the Brescia area, on-site presence is easier to arrange.

Do we need to buy software?

Hardly ever. In most cases we work with what you already have: your ERP, Excel and the Microsoft tools you are licensed for. If anything else is needed we say so upfront, with costs made clear.

Our data is confidential.

We sign a non-disclosure agreement before receiving any file. Data stays on your systems whenever that is technically possible.

What happens after the four months?

You decide. Some continue with a smaller monthly commitment to keep the system alive, some run it in-house, some start a new project in another area. No automatic renewal.
